MASTER SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Software Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between:

(1) NORTHSTAR RETAIL PTE. LTD., a company incorporated in Singapore
(“Client”); and

(2) VECTOR CLOUD SYSTEMS PTE. LTD., a company incorporated in Singapore
(“Service Provider”).

The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Services” means the software development, implementation, hosting,
maintenance and support services described in an applicable Statement of Work.

1.2 “Deliverables” means software, documentation, configurations and other
materials specifically identified as deliverables in a Statement of Work.

1.3 “Acceptance” means written confirmation by Client that a Deliverable
materially conforms to the applicable specifications.

1.4 “Business Day” means a day on which banks are open for business in
Singapore.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Service Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the
applicable Statement of Work.

2.2 Service Provider may use subcontractors to perform portions of the
Services, provided that Service Provider remains responsible for their acts
and omissions.

2.3 Client shall provide reasonable cooperation and access to information
required for Service Provider to perform the Services.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Client shall pay the fees specified in each Statement of Work.

3.2 For the initial implementation, Client shall pay SGD 250,000 upon
completion of the first milestone.

3.3 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days after receipt.

3.4 If Client disputes an invoice in good faith, Client may withhold the
disputed amount until the dispute is resolved.

3.5 All completed milestones shall be deemed accepted for payment purposes
unless Client provides written objections within five (5) Business Days.

4. ACCEPTANCE

4.1 Client shall review each Deliverable within ten (10) Business Days after
delivery.

4.2 If a Deliverable does not materially conform to the agreed specifications,
Client shall provide written notice describing the material deficiencies.

4.3 Service Provider shall correct material deficiencies and resubmit the
Deliverable for review.

4.4 If Client does not respond within ten (10) Business Days, the Deliverable
shall be deemed accepted.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Client owns all intellectual property rights in Deliverables specifically
created for Client and paid for by Client.

5.2 Service Provider retains all rights in its pre-existing software,
libraries, frameworks, tools, know-how and generic components.

5.3 To the extent Service Provider materials are incorporated into a
Deliverable, Service Provider grants Client a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive licence to use such materials as part of the Deliverable.

5.4 Client may modify, reproduce and distribute Deliverables internally.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received
from the other party in connection with this Agreement.

6.2 Confidential information may be disclosed to employees, professional
advisers and subcontractors who have a need to know such information and are
bound by confidentiality obligations.

6.3 These confidentiality obligations shall continue for two (2) years after
termination of this Agreement.

6.4 Information that is independently developed without use of the other
party's confidential information shall not be considered confidential.

7. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

7.1 Service Provider shall implement reasonable technical and organisational
measures to protect Client Data against unauthorised access, disclosure,
alteration or destruction.

7.2 Service Provider shall notify Client of a confirmed security incident
affecting Client Data without undue delay after becoming aware of the
incident.

7.3 Service Provider may process Client Data through hosting infrastructure
located outside Singapore where reasonably required to provide the Services.

7.4 Client is responsible for ensuring that it has all necessary rights and
permissions to provide Client Data to Service Provider.

8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Service Provider warrants that the Services shall be performed with
reasonable skill and care.

8.2 Service Provider warrants that the Deliverables will materially conform
to the applicable specifications for thirty (30) days after Acceptance.

8.3 The warranties in this Section are the exclusive warranties provided by
Service Provider in connection with the Services.

9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Each party shall indemnify the other party against third-party claims
arising from its breach of this Agreement.

9.2 Service Provider shall defend and indemnify Client against third-party
claims alleging that a Deliverable infringes a third party's intellectual
property rights.

9.3 The indemnified party shall promptly notify the indemnifying party of
any claim and provide reasonable cooperation.

9.4 The indemnifying party shall control the defence and settlement of the
claim, provided that it shall not settle any claim in a manner admitting
liability by or imposing non-monetary obligations on the indemnified party
without that party's consent.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Except for amounts payable under Section 9, each party's aggregate
liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not
exceed the total fees paid or payable during the twelve (12) months
preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

10.2 Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or
consequential loss, including loss of profits, revenue or anticipated
savings.

10.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability to the extent such limitation
is prohibited by applicable law.

11. TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2)
years unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.

11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the
breach remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

11.3 Client may terminate a Statement of Work for convenience upon thirty (30)
days' written notice.

11.4 Upon termination, Client shall pay Service Provider for all Services
performed and Deliverables completed up to the effective date of termination.

11.5 Sections 5, 6, 9 and 10 shall survive termination.

12. NOTICES

12.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be effective when sent by email
to the email address designated by the receiving party.

12.2 A party may change its designated email address by giving notice under
this Section.

12.3 Notices relating to termination shall be copied to the other party's
contract manager.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute
through discussions between senior representatives.

13.2 If the dispute has not been resolved within thirty (30) days, either
party may commence proceedings in the courts of Singapore.

13.3 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent interim
relief where necessary to protect its rights.

14. GENERAL

14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written
consent of the other party, except to an affiliate or in connection with a
merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all of its assets.

14.2 This Agreement and each Statement of Work constitute the entire
agreement between the parties concerning their subject matter.

14.3 If there is a conflict between this Agreement and a Statement of Work,
the Statement of Work shall prevail with respect to the Services described
in that Statement of Work.

14.4 No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless made in writing and
signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

14.5 A failure or delay in exercising a right does not constitute a waiver
of that right.

15. GOVERNING LAW

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Singapore.
